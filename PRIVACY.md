# Privacy Policy — TierFill

_Last updated: 2026_

TierFill does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

- **No data collection.** The extension does not gather any information about you or your
  browsing.
- **No tracking or analytics.** There are no analytics, telemetry, cookies, or identifiers of any
  kind.
- **No external servers.** The extension makes no network requests. All logic and tier data are
  bundled with the extension and run entirely within your own browser on the Path of Exile 2 trade
  page.
- **No accounts.** No login, registration, or personal information is required or requested.

The extension only reads and writes the trade site's own filter inputs on
`pathofexile.com/trade2` in order to fill in tier values for you. Nothing leaves your device.

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted in this file in the project
repository.

**Contact:** open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
